=================================================
OIL SPILL DETECTION DATASETS METADATA & CONVENTIONS
=================================================

This directory contains four primary datasets separated by their annotation format: Bounding Box (Object Detection) and Semantic Segmentation (Pixel-wise Masks).

-------------------------------------------------
1. BOUNDING BOX DATASET(S)
-------------------------------------------------

Folder: \Bounding Box\DARTIS
*   Origin: Dataset of Artificial and Real Threats of Oil Spills (DARTIS) / Trujillo-Acatitla et al. (2019/2024).
*   Format: Pascal VOC (.xml bounding boxes + .jpg images).
*   Classes: Contains only the 'oil' object class.

Naming Convention in DARTIS (e.g., nc-0001-00-000001.jpg):
The prefix represents what is in the image and its geographical context:
- First Letter (Presence of Oil): 
    'o' = Oil (contains an oil spill)
    'n' = No-Oil (contains clear water or a look-alike)
- Second Letter (Geography): 
    'w' = Water (open sea)
    'c' = Coast (coastal area)
- Therefore:
    ow = Oil in open Water
    oc = Oil near the Coast
    nw = No-oil in open Water
    nc = No-oil near the Coast

-------------------------------------------------
2. SEMANTIC SEGMENTATION DATASET(S)
-------------------------------------------------

Folder: \Semantic Segmentation\MADOS
*   Origin: Marine Artificial Dataset of Oil Spills (MADOS).
*   Sensor Modality: Multispectral / EO (Sentinel-2).
*   Format: Pixel-wise class masks (.tif or .png).
*   Classes: Originally 15 classes (mapped locally to OilSpill, LookAlikes, NoOilSpill based on semantic analysis). 
*   Metadata files: Includes 'rgb' (true color), 'cl' (class label masks), 'conf' (confidence map), 'rep' (representativeness).

Folder: \Semantic Segmentation\Sentinel-1_SAR_Oil_spill_image_dataset
*   Origin: Trujillo-Acatitla et al. (Parts I, II, III).
*   Sensor Modality: SAR (Sentinel-1).
*   Format: Binary pixel-wise masks for OilSpill, LookAlikes, and NoOilSpill.
*   Data Split: Combines Training and Validation/Test imagery into a strictly standardized folder structure.

Folder: \Semantic Segmentation\dataset (M4D Group Dataset)
*   Origin: Multimodal Data Fusion and Analytics (M4D) Group.
*   Sensor Modality: SAR (Sentinel-1).
*   Format: RGB Semantic Masks.
*   Class Mapping (RGB values):
    - [255,   0, 124] : Oil Spill
    - [255, 204,  51] : Others / LookAlikes
    - [ 51, 221, 255] : Water / NoOilSpill
    - [  0,   0,   0] : Background
